### ALGO-000056: Two-source extraction at the optimal leading entropy constant
- **Subfield:** Pseudorandomness, derandomization, and Boolean analysis
- **Open:** Construct a polynomial-time explicit one-bit extractor for two independent \(n\)-bit sources of min-entropy \(\log_2 n+O(1)\), with a fixed small error such as 0.01.
- **Status:** Li’s 2023 work achieved \(O(\log n)\) entropy. The unresolved target here is the optimal leading constant and additive gap, not merely logarithmic entropy.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Approaches the information-theoretic limit of extracting randomness from extremely weak independent sources.
- **Key works:** Li (2023), *Two Source Extractors for Asymptotically Optimal Entropy, and (Many) More*, arXiv:2303.06802, DOI: 10.1109/FOCS57990.2023.00075.
